# YupCrew: six-week marketing and advertising plan

**Positioning update, 8 September 2026:** **YupCrew** is the public brand, with `https://yupcrew.com` as the canonical address. See the [current rebrand direction](yupcrew-rebrand-plan.md). The product supports **Eat Out, Takeaway, Cook and Watch**. Test occasion-specific messages organically, then select one Branch for a focused paid flight only after the entry gates below pass. The A$500 envelope, six-week sequence, audience and channel choices remain learning hypotheses, not authorised spending or a committed launch schedule.

Researched 8 September 2026. Start date remains to be agreed. Owner: Zac. Planning assumption: up to A$500 cash and roughly 5–7 founder hours per week. Budget is provisional, not authorised expenditure. Nothing has been posted, purchased or launched.

## Recommendation

Start in Melbourne with couples who struggle to choose dinner. Recruit a small pilot directly, publish short videos showing two people actually using YupCrew, and test paid distribution through Meta only after the basic journey works for newcomers. Compare Eat Out, Takeaway, Cook and Watch demonstrations organically. Choose one Branch for the first paid destination based on observed completed group decisions and follow-through; Eat Out is an initial candidate, not a predetermined winner.

The immediate objective is evidence that strangers can reach a useful decision and households return. It is not maximum traffic. No source establishes which channel will convert best for YupCrew; the ranking below is a product-specific hypothesis to test, not a claim of proven acquisition economics.

## What the research changes

1. **The old name explains the rebrand.** A separate [Dinder](https://www.dinder.food/) markets restaurant swiping and shared invite links. [Dinnder](https://www.dinnder.app/) markets group restaurant decisions and links to an App Store listing. This creates observable search and word-of-mouth confusion. YupCrew is now selected; before buying reach, check its search results, social handles and [IP Australia's trade mark search tools](https://www.ipaustralia.gov.au/trade-marks/search-existing-trade-marks) against the selected public name. No trade mark clearance or ownership conclusion has been established by this research. A descriptor can improve clarity but does not resolve a rights conflict.
2. **“Swipe for dinner” is familiar territory.** [WFD](https://whatsfordinner.uk/couples) already advertises couple matching, weekly planning and shopping lists. Compete through a clear demonstration of YupCrew's guest-friendly browser flow and a useful outcome. Do not claim uniqueness.
3. **Meta is a reasonable first distribution test.** ACMA reports personal use during the six months to June 2025 of Facebook by 78% of Australian adults, Instagram by 53%, and TikTok by 30%. These are survey usage figures, not ad reach or conversion rates. They support testing Meta, without proving it wins. [ACMA, February 2026 report](https://www.acma.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-02/How%20we%20communicate%20-%20Executive%20summary%20and%20key%20findings.pdf), © Commonwealth of Australia (Australian Communications and Media Authority) 2026.
4. **Paid TikTok is awkward at this budget.** Its documented ad-group daily minimum is above US$20; a five-day lifetime group requires at least US$100. Those amounts are USD, not AUD. Organic TikTok can still reuse the videos. See the [channel research](marketing-channel-research.md) for sources and configuration limitations.
5. **Communities offer access, not permission to advertise.** UMSU's current directory lists Consistently Amazing Kitchen Endeavours, Healthy Eating and Nutrition Society, and other food clubs. These are concrete pilot-recruitment leads, particularly for housemates; none has agreed to participate. [UMSU directory](https://umsu.unimelb.edu.au/buddy-up/clubs/clubs-listing/). Reddit prohibits repeated unsolicited promotion and directs people to check community rules. Do not assume r/melbourne allows this post. [Reddit spam policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam).

## Audience, promise and destination

Primary research audience: Melbourne couples, initially around ages 25–44, who regularly defer dinner decisions. Secondary pilot audience: two-to-four-person shared households. Age and segment are hypotheses; recruit across genders and do not infer relationship or dietary status from ad targeting.

**Brand message:** “What are we doing tonight?”

| Branch | Organic message hypothesis | Existing entry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eat Out | “Both hungry. Different ideas. Pick somewhere together.” | `/create?branch=eatout` |
| Takeaway | “Staying in? Choose takeaway together.” | `/create?branch=takeaway` |
| Cook | “You’re both cooking. You both get a say.” | `/cook` |
| Watch | “Still scrolling while the popcorn goes cold?” | `/watch` |

These are test messages, not claims of measured performance.

**Supporting proof:** “Open a link. Invite your people. Everyone swipes. See your shared picks. No download or account needed.”

The browser/guest claim is supported by the current app and [ADR 0003](adr/0003-auth-is-optional-guests-are-first-class.md). Show the actual Ready, swipe and submission steps in the demonstration; do not imply an instant automatic Match. YupCrew can produce no unanimous Match, so avoid promises that everyone will agree every time.

Once a Branch is selected, use one focused mobile landing page. For an Eat Out test, the example is proposed at `/dinner` (not implemented), with:

- That headline, a short real two-phone demonstration, and the guest/no-download benefit.
- One main CTA: **“Choose where to eat”**, opening the existing `/create?branch=eatout` route.
- A smaller **“Joining someone?”** link to `/join`.
- A plain explanation that both people participate on their phones; a visible link to the wider app.

This gives a cold visitor context before asking for a name. Avoid a four-branch advertising menu, email gate or app-store button. Until the page exists, use the current home page for direct pilot recruitment; do not advertise a nonexistent route. Verify the chosen destination’s current social preview during campaign preparation and make its copy match the advertised occasion.

For the first test, avoid unsupported “decide in 30 seconds”, guaranteed savings, allergy-safety or “cheapest delivery” claims. YupCrew's comparison does not establish every person's final checkout price. Show real usage and accurately label demonstrations.

## Channel priorities

| Channel | Role now | Execution | Decision criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personal introductions | First 10–15 pilot households | Invite people who experience the problem; observe five first sessions | Useful decisions and unprompted return |
| Instagram Reels; reuse on TikTok | Test messages with real demonstrations | Three original clips weekly, exported without another platform's watermark | Qualified visits and completed group sessions, not views alone |
| Meta Ads Manager | First paid test | One Melbourne audience, two or three creatives, two sequential capped flights | Cost per completed group session and qualitative follow-through |
| Relevant local communities/clubs | Small supplementary pilot source | Ask organisers first; one transparent founder post where permitted | Households recruited, completed sessions, feedback |
| Local creators | Learn and source credible demonstrations | Shortlist five Melbourne couple/food creators; approach only through appropriate business channels | Local audience fit and attributable group sessions |
| Google Search | Later intent test | Validate search volume and forecasts in Keyword Planner first | Enough relevant demand at an affordable forecast cost |
| Paid TikTok, broad PR, Product Hunt | Defer | Revisit after message and repeat use are demonstrated | A specific acquisition hypothesis worth funding |

For creators, review their latest ten relevant posts, the substance of comments, typical views and Melbourne/Australian audience evidence they can supply. Follower count alone is insufficient. Request a quote for one two-person demo and separately for paid reuse rights. The A$500 plan assumes founder-made creative; it does not assume a professional creator will work for A$50. Any paid/gifted collaboration must be clearly disclosed, and permission to run the content as an ad must be explicit; see the channel research's Australian sources.

Search candidates for a later test: “app to decide where to eat”, “restaurant picker for couples”, “can't decide where to eat Melbourne”. Inspect Keyword Planner before spending; volume and CPC are unverified. Avoid broad “restaurants Melbourne” initially because someone wanting a restaurant listing may not want a shared decision tool. Use Melbourne presence targeting rather than Google's default presence-or-interest setting. Do not fund multiple paid channels at once with A$300.

## Six-week execution

Weeks are relative to an agreed start, not permission to advance past a failed gate. A missed prerequisite pushes the paid schedule back.

| Week | Work | Concrete output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Check YupCrew landing/share readiness; arrange 10–15 pilot households; observe five two-phone sessions; establish measurement and landing-page requirements | Five observation notes, a baseline funnel, three recorded clips; all four occasion hooks represented across the first two weeks |
| 2 | Publish three clips; select the strongest Branch hypothesis; obtain permission for up to three community approaches; fix the largest joining/decision obstacle; let pilot members return without prompts | Ten completed first-session observations in total; first eligible seven-day return cohort |
| 3 | If entry gates pass, run the first five-day Meta flight; keep organic output at three clips; inspect results daily for broken delivery or flow | Up to A$150 media spent, source-attributed completed sessions, a documented bottleneck |
| 4 | Change one diagnosed variable—hook, landing page or journey; run second five-day flight only if the first merits it | Up to a further A$150 media spent; comparable directional results |
| 5 | Interview early users after the return-observation window; publish a consented genuine user demonstration; explore one creator collaboration if justified | Follow-through evidence, early 28-day pilot return results, creator quote if useful |
| 6 | Review mature cohorts and both ad flights; decide whether to fund the next test, fix the product or change the audience | One-page decision with spend, session outcomes, return counts and next hypothesis |

Weekly founder time: approximately two hours filming/editing, one hour posting/responding, two hours research sessions and follow-up, one hour reviewing evidence. Product fixes or measurement implementation are additional engineering work, not hidden inside this estimate. Conduct retention interviews after the measurement window, or record earlier contact as prompting.

## Advertising setup and spend

Provisional all-in cash ceiling: **A$500**, subject to the user's budget choice.

| Envelope | Amount | Conditions |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Meta flight 1 | A$150 | Five days; only after entry gates |
| Meta flight 2 | A$150 | Five days; only after first-flight review |
| Research thank-yous | A$100 | Five A$20 interview payments; unrelated to positive feedback or repeat usage |
| Asset expenses | A$50 | Optional; use existing phones and free editing tools first |
| Reserve | A$50 | Taxes, transaction costs or contingency; leave unspent unless needed |
| **Total** | **A$500** | No ongoing commitment |

Media envelopes include any applicable tax/fees: reduce the configured platform spend if necessary to stay inside them. Paid creator fees and professional name/legal work are not included. Research compensation is not payment for testimonials; record which participants received it.

In desktop/browser Ads Manager, use one website Traffic campaign initially, one ad set, Melbourne metro, ages 25–44 as a starting hypothesis, all genders, no layered food/relationship interests. Use mobile Feed/Reels/Stories placements on Facebook and Instagram, checking each preview. Start with two or three videos using the same landing page and branch. Check location-control/expansion settings in the live account; location delivery is approximate. Do not split the small budget into many audiences.

Prefer landing-page-view optimisation only if supported and its measurement is correctly configured. If it requires third-party tracking that is not ready, use link clicks with first-party downstream measurement. Neither objective means Meta is optimising for a successful group decision. Treat flight 1 as a traffic-quality and message test. Website conversion optimisation becomes a later experiment once a meaningful event is safely measurable and occurs regularly. Do not invent purchases, registrations or app installs to fit an objective.

Use a five-day lifetime budget and an account/campaign spending limit where available; confirm currency, minimum, end date and fees before launch. Current Meta Help documentation was login-blocked during research, so live account validation is required; A$150 is a proposed envelope, not a verified platform minimum. If the chosen setup cannot respect it, keep the cash and continue organically. Do not substitute an open-ended daily budget.

A starting organic posting window is 4–7 pm Melbourne time, as a dinner-occasion hypothesis. Do not impose a narrow paid delivery window on the first tiny test. Publish early enough to catch people before they have chosen dinner; compare actual results before declaring a best posting time.

## Creative pack

Film vertical phone video with readable captions and real app footage. Keep faces/text inside placement-safe areas. Use original audio or music cleared for advertising. For the paid flight, make three hooks around the selected Branch so a Branch difference does not masquerade as a creative result. The scripts below illustrate an Eat Out flight.

**Video A — the familiar conversation, 20 seconds**

- 0–3s: Two people: “Where do you want to eat?” / “What are you thinking?” Caption: “Dinner planning, again.”
- 3–7s: Show opening YupCrew and sharing the invite link. Caption: “Send one link.”
- 7–15s: Show both phones, Ready and Like/Pass, then actual submission and shared picks. Caption: “Both get a say.”
- 15–20s: Show the chosen restaurant and next action. Caption: “Try it together tonight. No download.”

**Video B — the product proof**

Hook: “We each picked where we'd eat. Here's what overlapped.” Show an actual session with different initial preferences and its honest outcome. Avoid a fabricated timer or fake testimonial.

**Video C — the founder invitation**

Hook: “I built this in Melbourne because choosing dinner became a second job.” Continue only if that origin story is true; otherwise say “I'm testing a simple way for two people to choose dinner.” Show the two-phone flow. Close: “Try it with someone you eat with and tell me where you get stuck.”

**Ad text:** “Different dinner ideas? Open YupCrew, send your partner a link and both swipe on nearby restaurants. See what you both like. No download or account needed.”

**Headline:** “Pick dinner together.” **Button:** “Learn more”, or the closest available accurate website CTA. Do not say “Install”. Put the actual domain in the final video frame using `yupcrew.com` consistently.

The scripts above are Eat Out examples. If Takeaway, Cook or Watch wins the organic test, adapt the demonstration, CTA and destination to that Branch before preparing the paid flight.

These are drafts, not messages sent. No scraped mailing lists or mass DMs. Use explicit opt-in for a marketing mailing list; pilot participation is not blanket permission for promotional follow-ups.

## Measurement and decisions

The original code review found no dedicated marketing attribution/funnel instrumentation; recheck the current implementation before campaign preparation. App-store installs are the wrong unit for this browser product. Tags alone do not create analytics: implement and verify attribution before paid tests, or defer them.

Use campaign tags such as `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=melbourne_dinner_pilot&utm_content=conversation_a`. Keep acquisition attribution separate from the invitee's referral source. Attribute a completed Session to the initiating host's campaign; a partner opening its invite is not a second paid acquisition.

Measure this sequence:

| Measure | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Landing visits | Measured visits, distinct from the platform's reported clicks; document bot/QA exclusions |
| Session created | Successful host creation, not a CTA click |
| Two participants joined | The Session contains at least two participants; exclude known tests |
| Swiping started | The group starts its first round |
| Completed group session | At least two participants complete a round; count once per Session, excluding Restarts and known tests |
| Next action | Maps/delivery handoff, recipe/list action or Watch provider/trailer action; a proxy, not proof of follow-through |
| Actual follow-through | Pilot participant reports that they ate/ordered/cooked/watched a choice from the Session |
| Seven-day / 28-day return | A consenting pilot household uses YupCrew on a later distinct day within the window, without a reminder; report counts and denominator |

Cost per completed group session = attributable media spend divided by campaign-attributed completed group sessions. **This is not customer acquisition cost:** anonymous guests cannot be reliably deduplicated into new households across Sessions. Also report total cash and founder time. Without a revenue model and measured retention, no profitable CAC ceiling is known.

Keep cross-session household tracking in a separate consenting pilot roster for now. [ADR 0001](adr/0001-ephemeral-sessions-in-redis-social-graph-in-supabase.md) expressly rules out durable Session history; [ADR 0003](adr/0003-auth-is-optional-guests-are-first-class.md) treats remembered identity as an optional Profile concern. Document any aggregate funnel-measurement boundary before implementation; do not quietly retain raw Sessions or identify every guest to satisfy ad attribution.

Do not place broad automatic ad pixels on Session, preference or invite pages: names, dietary choices, locations and live Session Codes should not become ad-platform data. A separate landing-page-only configuration is an option after reviewing exactly what it sends and providing the appropriate notice/controls. [OAIC's pixel guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/organisations/tracking-pixels-and-privacy-obligations) describes data minimisation, transparency and sensitive-information concerns; applicability and configuration need assessment rather than assuming an analytics script is harmless.

### Proposed gates — hypotheses, not industry benchmarks

- **Before paid flight 1:** at least 8 of 10 observed first household sessions complete without assistance; at least 6 of those 10 report following through; at least 5 of the first 10 eligible households return unprompted within seven days. Observe honestly: hints from the founder make a session assisted. Verify the landing-to-completion measurement end to end.
- **During a flight:** pause immediately for a broken destination/join flow or broken attribution. At A$75 media spend with zero completed group sessions, stop and diagnose. This is a loss limit, not statistically conclusive rejection of the market.
- **Before flight 2:** seek at least 10 completed group sessions from flight 1's A$150 envelope, alongside evidence people can act on the choice. If below this, diagnose the funnel and change one thing before deciding whether a second test is worthwhile. A$15 per completed session is an experimental ceiling, not a profitable CAC claim.
- **At week 6:** seek at least 5 of the original 10 eligible pilot households returning during days 22–28. Report seven-day return separately. Late cohorts have not all matured; do not count them as failures or fabricate paid 28-day retention. Interview people who stopped as well as those who stayed.
- **Fund the next bounded test** only if completion, follow-through and repeat-use evidence are encouraging. With ten households, show counts rather than claiming statistical certainty. Compare channels directionally; Meta will not evenly allocate impressions across creatives, and sequential flights are not a controlled A/B test.

Diagnostic guide: views without visits suggest the hook attracts entertainment rather than intent; visits without creation suggest unclear promise or entry friction; creation without a second participant suggests invitation/coordination friction; groups without completion suggest setup or Deck problems; completed Sessions without follow-through suggest unsuitable options; no return suggests low recurring value. Investigate these explanations rather than treating them as automatic diagnoses.

## Zero-spend option and what follows

With A$0, keep the same six-week structure, recruit ten volunteer households, make the videos on existing phones, and use permitted community posts and referrals. Replace both ad flights with repeated organic message tests. Skip compensation and paid assets; expect slower and more biased recruitment. Obtain the same completion and return evidence before paying for reach.

If the budget grows to A$1,500, do not distribute it across platforms immediately. Keep the A$500 first stage; release another A$500 for the best-supported message and audience only after the gates, and hold A$500 for a quoted creator experiment or a Keyword Planner-supported Search test. Quote fees and rights rather than assuming creator market prices.

First actions: check YupCrew campaign readiness, recruit the pilot, record Video A, and establish the landing-to-completed-session baseline. More reach becomes useful once this sequence produces decisions people act on and repeat.

## Evidence limits

This is primary-source desk research plus a review of the repository. Competitor pages establish positioning and advertised functionality, not actual traction. No production analytics, ad account forecasts, creator quotes, live campaign settings, trade mark search result, or fresh production two-phone test was available. Meta Help pages required login. Budget allocations and thresholds are planning choices; no CPC, CPM, conversion-rate or revenue forecast has been invented. Platform setup may change and should be checked at launch.
