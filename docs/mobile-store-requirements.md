# Mobile store requirements research

Verified **8 September 2026** against the official sources linked below. This supports the mobile conversion plan; it does not certify a finished binary or account. Scope: a **free, Australia-first** iPhone/Android release of Dinder, currently being renamed **Heykeen**. Final brand, domain, publishing entity, account status, and launch age audience remain decisions to confirm. The current app uses optional **Google OAuth**, not email-only authentication, according to `frontend/src/services/supabase.ts` inspected by the planning task.

## Release gates and applicability

| Gate | Requirement and action |
| --- | --- |
| Publishing identity | Choose the actual legal owner; complete both enrollments and verification. Do this early because it can run alongside engineering. See enrollment below. |
| Current toolchains | Build iOS with the iOS 26 SDK or later; target Android 16/API 36 or later. These requirements already apply on this note's date. |
| Authentication | Google OAuth brings Apple's equivalent-login rule into scope. Plan Sign in with Apple unless the product deliberately changes its account system. |
| Account removal | Provide complete account/data deletion and a separate web request route. Sign-out or deleting local storage does not satisfy this. |
| Social safety | Treat shared names/profiles as UGC; verify all surfaces and implement proportionate prevention, reporting, blocking, and operator response. |
| Privacy | Complete a real data inventory, public policy, store disclosures, permission explanations, and native SDK/privacy-manifest audit. |
| Reviewable product | Ship a reliable installed experience and provide reviewers access to multi-person and authenticated flows without assistance. |
| Production access | If the Play owner has a personal account created after 13 November 2023, budget the mandatory closed test and subsequent production-access review. |

These are planning gates; their supporting rules and qualifications follow. Store acceptance remains a review decision.

## Enrollment, ownership, and costs

- **Apple:** US$99 per membership year, with regional/local-currency pricing. Individuals and sole proprietors appear under their personal legal name. Organizations need a qualifying legal entity, D-U-N-S number, binding authority, domain-associated work email, and functional public website. A trading name alone does not establish organization eligibility. Enrollment also requires an Apple Account with two-factor authentication. Choose the seller identity before registering identifiers or uploading builds. [Apple enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/program-enrollment)
- **Google:** US$25 one-time registration fee; account owner must be at least 18. Select the account type matching the actual owner, accept the distribution agreement, and complete identity/contact verification. [Play Console registration](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6112435?hl=en-EN-2), [account types](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13634885?hl=en)
- **Google public details:** personal accounts expose legal name, country, and developer email; monetization can add the full address. Organization accounts expose legal name/address, developer email, and developer phone. Organizations normally need D-U-N-S; Google says obtaining one can take up to 30 days. Use durable support contacts and matching legal records. [Required account information](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312?hl=en)
- **New personal Play account:** verify access to a non-rooted physical Android device running Android 10 or later using the Play Console app. This is separate from closed testing. Existing account eligibility has not been inspected. [Device verification](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14316361?hl=en)

No accounts, purchases, certificates, or production settings were changed for this research. Quoted fees exclude hardware, local taxes/exchange effects, CI, and hosting.

## Review acceptance and account access

Apple 4.2 requires utility beyond a repackaged website. Apple 4.8 requires an equivalent login that limits collection to name/email, supports private email, and avoids advertising interaction collection without consent when third-party login authenticates the primary account. Exclusively first-party sign-in has an exception. Apple 1.2 requires UGC filtering, reporting with timely response, blocking, and published contact details. [Apple review guidelines: 4.2, 4.8, 1.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)

**Application to this app:** retain and demonstrate its core group decision flow; improve installed behavior through reliable invitation links, sharing, safe areas, keyboard/back navigation, and resume/reconnect handling. These are product recommendations, not an official checklist or approval guarantee. Keep Google sign-in only if an equivalent compliant iOS option is added; Sign in with Apple is the straightforward implementation choice. Optional login does not by itself remove the rule when it creates the account used for Friends.

Google requires stable, responsive, useful functionality. Its WebView policy prohibits unauthorized website wrappers and apps primarily driving affiliate traffic; it does not categorically prohibit an owner distributing their web app in a native container. A working interactive app still has to pass quality and policy review. [Functionality policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898783?hl=en), [WebViews and spam](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9899034?hl=en)

Prepare working reviewer access, clear review notes, and live backend services. For this app, document how to create a Session, add another Participant, reach a Match, and exercise Friends and deletion. Supply reproducible Australia-location inputs for reviewers abroad. Default to ordinary dedicated review accounts and separate disposable deletion fixtures. Apple's full-demo-mode substitution under guideline 2.1(a) applies when legal/security obligations prevent account access and requires prior Apple approval; it is not an unconditional alternative. Play requires reusable access unaffected by reviewer location and instructions for social-login or OTP restrictions. Prove repeatability after deletion and social-provider challenges before submission; do not create a hidden authorization bypass. [Apple App Review](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/), [Apple guideline 2.1(a)](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), [Google sign-in requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/15748846?hl=en-EN)

## Account deletion and social safety

**Apple:** apps supporting account creation must let users initiate deletion inside the app, including accounts created by linking to a website. Delete the account and associated personal data, except justified legal retention. Make the option easy to find; deactivation is insufficient. Ordinary apps cannot substitute a customer-support call/email requirement. If Sign in with Apple is added, revoke its tokens during deletion. [Apple deletion guidance](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app)

**Google:** provide both an in-app deletion path and a functional public web resource for requesting account/data deletion without reinstalling the app. Enter that URL and deletion answers in Play Console. The page must identify the app/developer and make the request path prominent. Disclose any justified retention. [Google deletion requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en-EN)

**Implementation acceptance:** verify removal of the authentication record, profile, friendships/requests, linked saved data, and attributable session data as appropriate to the app's data model; invalidate sessions and clear device credentials. Document logs/backups, retention windows, and exceptions accurately. Test deletion from a fresh account through completion, including subsequent sign-in. Do not delete another participant's independent records.

Google defines UGC to include user submissions visible to even a subset of users. It calls for accepted terms before contribution, defined prohibited behavior, proportionate moderation, in-app reporting, and blocking for 1:1 interactions; publicly accessible UGC needs reporting of content/users and user blocking. [Google UGC policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937?hl=en-GB)

**Inference for Dinder/Heykeen:** display names and Profile details visible in Sessions/Friends qualify for UGC assessment despite private groups and absence of chat. Independently shared Shopping Lists expose Shopper names after Session expiry, including Shoppers who were never Participants. Include them in terms, validation, reporting and scoped content controls. Combine local content muting with enforceable Profile blocks, revocation of removed Participant capabilities and operator remediation; muting alone is not a complete moderation system. A self-declared name is not a durable identity, so do not promise person-level exclusion after someone creates a fresh anonymous identity. Record an operator response process, abuse contact and minimal report retention; durable user-submitted evidence requires an explicit exception to the repository's normal no-Session-history rule. Exact moderation scope remains dependent on the shipped surfaces; no exemption has been confirmed.

## Privacy, SDKs, and permissions

Prepare one data-flow inventory before answering store forms: name/email/avatar and auth identifiers; social graph; room names, participants, votes, and history; approximate/precise location; dietary choices; device storage; IP/access/error logs; and any analytics/crash SDK. Record collection purpose, linkage, recipients, retention, deletion, and optionality. Confirm what Railway, Supabase, Redis, Cloudflare, Google services, image hosts, and any newly added native plugins actually receive. This inventory is a task list, not a claim that every listed category is presently collected.

- **Apple privacy labels:** include relevant collection by third-party partners, collection purpose, linkage to identity, and tracking. Collection for core functionality still counts. Apple's real-time-processing definition differs from durable collection; inspect logging and retention rather than assuming ephemeral rooms mean no collection. Update disclosures when behavior changes. [App privacy details](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/)
- **Google Data safety:** covers app-controlled WebViews and SDKs, not just native code. Its primary instructions require ephemeral off-device processing in form responses, even when excluded from the displayed section; a later FAQ on the same page has less consistent wording. Follow the live form and main definition, documenting the reasoning. Service-provider transfers and other sharing exceptions also need classification. [Data safety guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en)
- **Privacy policy:** publish accessible HTML linked in-app and from store fields. Google requires an active, public, non-geofenced, non-PDF policy, including developer/contact information, data uses/recipients, handling, retention, and deletion, even for apps with no personal-data collection. Unexpected sensitive-data use can require prominent in-app disclosure and affirmative consent beyond the policy. [Google User Data policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?hl=en)
- **Apple native privacy audit:** inspect app/plugin `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` files, required-reason API use, and approved reasons. SDKs must declare their own covered use; do not copy generic reasons without matching behavior. **Capacitor is explicitly on Apple's required SDK list**: its privacy manifest is required for new apps, and signatures are required when listed SDKs are binary dependencies. Check the archived binary and other dependencies too. These declarations do not replace store labels. [Required-reason APIs](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/describing-use-of-required-reason-api), [current SDK requirements and list](https://developer.apple.com/support/third-party-SDK-requirements/)
- **Location:** request access when the user chooses nearby restaurants; keep manual suburb/postcode entry and approximate-location support. Use foreground access only for this use case. iOS needs a clear `NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription`; Android needs the appropriate manifest/runtime permissions, with coarse and fine requested together if fine is necessary. Test refusal, restricted access, and later revocation. [Apple protected resources](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/requesting-access-to-protected-resources), [Android runtime location](https://developer.android.com/develop/sensors-and-location/location/permissions/runtime)
- **Additional capabilities:** contacts, photos, background location, notifications, or tracking are not prerequisites for store distribution. Add each only for a defined feature, with its permission/disclosure review. Free first release should not acquire an advertising SDK merely to obtain diagnostics. User-selected system sharing is sufficient for invitations unless evidence requires more.

## Content, encryption, payments, and audience

- **Rights:** Apple requires ownership or permission for included content and third-party services. [Review guideline 5.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) Audit recipe text/images, movie metadata/posters, restaurant photos, logos, fonts, and screenshots. Preserve an evidence record for rights and attribution rather than assuming existing web availability is permission for redistribution.
- **TMDB:** noncommercial API/data/image use is free with attribution; its definition of commercial concerns revenue purpose, not only a download charge. Use an approved logo and the required non-endorsement notice in About/Credits. Check licensing before introducing ads, paid features, or other revenue. [TMDB FAQ](https://developer.themoviedb.org/docs/faq)
- **Google Places:** retain required Google Maps and third-party attribution, photo/review author attribution, and applicable source links. Check current terms for the specific API in use before adding offline caching or displaying Google data on a different map. Current policy also requires public terms/privacy documents. [Places policies and attributions](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies)
- **Encryption:** answer Apple's export questions for the complete binary, including libraries, before TestFlight/review. OS-provided HTTPS encryption is typically exempt from documentation upload; that is not equivalent to claiming the app uses no encryption. Set `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` only after that determination. [Export compliance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/overview-of-export-compliance/), [encryption declaration](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/security/complying-with-encryption-export-regulations)
- **Payments:** no billing implementation is needed for the confirmed free release. If digital features are sold later, ordinary Apple/Google in-app billing rules apply subject to then-current market/program exceptions. Physical groceries/meals use other payment methods. Do not assume US/EU external-payment exceptions apply to an Australian release. [Apple 3.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), [Google Payments](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738?hl=en)
- **Audience:** choose target ages truthfully and complete current questionnaires. Google child-directed distribution has extra Families obligations. Movie selection does not justify a guessed rating; evaluate the actual posters, summaries, outbound links, and social features. Australia-only availability should be explicit in both consoles; expanding countries is a separate policy/licensing review. [Play app-content declarations](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859455?hl=en)
- **Store artwork:** Apple's guideline 2.3.8 requires metadata imagery suitable for a 4+ audience even if the app itself receives a higher rating; 2.3.9 calls for fictional account information in screenshots. Select suitable Watch artwork and fictional Profile, Participant and Shopper details for listing assets. This does not change the app's content rating. [Apple metadata rules](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)

## Signing, review assets, beta, and release operation

| Workstream | Required preparation |
| --- | --- |
| iOS build identity | Finalize bundle ID before first upload; select the publishing team, signing/capabilities, version/build numbers, app icon, launch screen, and supported destinations. Keep signing material in controlled account/CI storage. [Apple distribution preparation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/Xcode/preparing-your-app-for-distribution) |
| Android build identity | Use a stable application ID; publish a signed **AAB** with **Play App Signing**. Protect the separate upload keystore and credentials. Increment `versionCode` for updates; map release signing fingerprints to OAuth/App Links configuration. [AAB requirement](https://developer.android.com/guide/app-bundle), [signing](https://developer.android.com/studio/publish/app-signing), [upload/versioning](https://developer.android.com/studio/publish/upload-bundle), [App Links signing](https://developer.android.com/codelabs/android-app-links-introduction) |
| Store listings | Final name, accurate description/category, support/privacy URLs, copyright/rights, review contact/access notes, age/content/ads declarations, and real device screenshots. Capture all supported form factors. Apple accepts 1–10 screenshots; use its current size matrix. Play needs a 512×512 icon, 1024×500 feature graphic, and appropriate screenshots. [Apple screenshots](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications/), [Play assets](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en) |
| Apple beta | TestFlight supports up to 100 internal App Store Connect users and 10,000 external testers; builds expire after 90 days. The first external group build goes to review. No equivalent mandatory 12-person/14-day policy is stated here. [TestFlight overview](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/testflight-overview) |
| Google beta | If the owner meets the new-personal-account condition, keep **at least 12 testers opted in continuously for the preceding 14 days**, then apply for production access. Collect actual usage feedback, fixes, and readiness evidence; time/count alone does not guarantee access. Google's review usually takes seven days or less but may take longer or request more testing. [Personal-account testing](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en) |
| First public release | Use beta tracks as the gradual exposure mechanism, then release to the chosen Australian storefront audience after approval. Google percentage rollout is explicitly for updates, not first publication. Apple's phased option is also for version updates. [Play staged rollout](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6346149?hl=en), [Apple phased release](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/update-your-app/release-a-version-update-in-phases) |
| Later releases | Apple offers a seven-day automatic-update phase; manual downloads remain available. Google percentages require manual increase. Halting does not remove already installed bad versions. Keep backend compatibility with older clients and prepare a forward-fix build; Apple cannot revert the store to an earlier version. [Apple update recovery](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/update-your-app/create-a-new-version/) |
