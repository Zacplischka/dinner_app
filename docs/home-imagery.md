# Home imagery

The four `frontend/public/images/tonight-*.webp` assets were generated for Dinder with the built-in imagegen tool on 7 September 2026. They illustrate the four kinds of night, rather than representing a particular Restaurant, Recipe or Movie. No third-party source photo or attribution requirement was introduced.

The generated PNGs were encoded as WebP at quality 80, preserving their 1536 × 1024 dimensions and composition. Together the served assets are 435 KB. The original generated files remain outside the repository. The fixed image frames reserve space before loading; coloured surfaces and readable labels remain on failure.
