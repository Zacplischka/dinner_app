# Dinder advertising channel research

> Research snapshot from 8 September 2026, retained from the original Dinder draft. Current public direction and the four-Branch experiment are in the [YupCrew marketing plan](marketing-launch-plan.md). Source qualifications below remain intact; this cleanup did not reverify external policies, pricing or account settings.

Researched 8 September 2026. Scope: a Melbourne-first consumer **web** product, an assumed A$500 total learning budget, and sparse conversion data. Recommendations below are judgments to test, not claims of proven channel performance. No campaigns were created or launched.

## Recommendation

Use founder-made short videos, household recruitment and permission-based local community posts first. Reuse the strongest demonstrations in one small Meta website-traffic test. Keep TikTok organic initially. Defer Google Search until Keyword Planner shows relevant Melbourne demand. Do not divide A$300 of media across three advertising platforms.

Meta is the proposed first paid channel because a short demonstration can explain a shared dinner decision before someone knows to search for it. This is a product/channel-fit hypothesis; no Dinder account performance, local CPM/CPC forecasts, audience estimates or creator quotes were available.

## A bounded first Meta test

**Planning recommendation:** reserve up to A$300 media, released as two sequential A$150 flights of roughly five days, with the second conditional on useful first-flight results. Use one ad set and two or three real product demonstrations. Set start/end dates and use a lifetime budget after verifying the exact account controls and minimums. Treat any applicable taxes/fees as part of the overall authorised cash ceiling.

Current Meta minimums in AUD, exact daily-overdelivery rules, lifetime-cap wording and account-specific audience controls **were not verified**: the [minimum-budget help page](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/203183363050448), [objective help page](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1438417719786914) and [pricing page](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/pricing) required login. Do not present a US$1/day minimum or a particular overdelivery percentage as verified. Verify these settings in Ads Manager during campaign preparation; do not increase the proposed spending ceiling merely to satisfy a recommendation.

**Targeting recommendation:** Melbourne adults, broad within that geography, with couples/housemates self-selecting through the creative. Confirm the actual geographic control and preview all placements. Do not promise that postcode, resident-only or other fine-grained restrictions exist in the account without checking. Relationship-status targeting and multiple narrow interest audiences are unnecessary for this small test.

**Measure:** cost per first-party landing visit, created session, session with a second participant, and completed decision. Also ask a recruited pilot cohort whether they actually ate the selection and later returned. A cheap click or generated Top Pick alone is not success. Count a group session once, rather than counting each participant as a separate acquired household.

If landing-page-view optimisation needs third-party measurement that has not been reviewed, use an available click-based website objective with independent measurement, or postpone paid testing. Keep dietary preferences, names, invite codes and other session payloads away from advertising platforms. Do not add pixels throughout the app merely to run a small experiment. Conversion optimisation is a later choice once a meaningful event can be measured reliably and the account has enough observed activity to assess delivery; no universal numeric event threshold is claimed here.

## TikTok: organic first, paid later

The current official Traffic setup offers Website → Landing Page View; clicks are the default unless changed. A counted LPV requires both a click and a successfully loaded destination. TikTok says Pixel tracking is optional for this LPV setup. [TikTok LPV guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/landing-page-view?lang=en-GB)

Web-conversion campaigns require TikTok Pixel or Events API. Those tools measure website events; native app-install campaigns are a different route and are not the correct starting point for a browser product without an app-store listing. [TikTok Events API](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/events-api?lang=en)

Location options vary by market. TikTok targets people located in or regularly in selected areas, does not guarantee perfect accuracy, does not support location exclusions, and warns that tiny areas can reduce delivery and increase acquisition cost. Check whether Melbourne and the desired geographic granularity are available in the Australian account. [TikTok location targeting](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/location-targeting?lang=en)

If an organic creator demonstration brings qualified visitors, a later Spark Ads test can promote that existing post with the creator's authorisation. [TikTok Spark Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/spark-ads)

## Google Search: validate demand before allocating money

Check Keyword Planner for Melbourne intent around “choose dinner together”, “dinner decider”, “what should we eat app” and “restaurant decider”. These are proposed seed terms, not verified high-volume opportunities. Separate Cook and restaurant intent. Start with tightly relevant exact/phrase terms if useful demand exists, inspect actual search terms, and exclude irrelevant dating, employment and restaurant-operator queries as they appear.

Google's default geographic setting includes **Presence or Interest**. For a Melbourne pilot, select **Presence** (people in or regularly in the location). Google describes location as inferred and does not guarantee complete accuracy. [Google advanced location options](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722038?hl=en)

Google now documents total campaign budgets for Search with specified dates and states that billed campaign spend does not exceed the entered total. This is preferable to assuming an average daily budget is a hard daily limit. [Google campaign total-budget FAQ](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15137812?hl=en)

For a small diagnostic Search test, Maximize Clicks with an explicit CPC bid limit is one option; it optimises clicks, not retained users, and a low limit may restrict delivery. Use independently verified activation counts to judge the test. [Google Maximize Clicks](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6268626?hl=en)

Website conversion measurement uses a Google tag or linked Google Analytics events. It does not require treating the product as a native app. Reuse existing appropriate measurement rather than installing another analytics stack for a speculative channel. [Google web conversions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16560108?hl=en)

## Creator partnerships and Australian disclosure

Select small Melbourne creators for relevant audience and believable dinner/relationship content, not follower totals alone. Ask for location breakdown, typical views on comparable posts, a fee quote, deliverables and separate organic-reuse/paid-ad rights. Rates and availability remain unknown; the A$500 starter plan should not promise a paid creator package.

Australian consumer-law obligations apply to both influencers and brands. The ACCC calls for immediately obvious disclosure where payment, gifts or other incentives are involved, and truthful descriptions of actual experience. AANA's self-regulatory code separately requires commercial relationships to be clear and upfront; labels such as “gifted”, “collab” or abbreviated “spon” may be insufficient. Use conspicuous **Ad / Paid partnership** in the content and caption, plus the platform's disclosure tools; do not hide disclosure after a caption expansion. [ACCC guidance](https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/scrutiny-of-influencers-and-businesses-for-misleading-advertising-and-online-reviews-continues), [AANA Code of Ethics](https://aana.com.au/self-regulation/code-of-ethics/)

Agree in writing on the fee, deliverables, posting date, disclosure, editing rights, reuse duration and separate paid promotion permission. Do not script a fabricated testimonial or a performance claim such as “always decides dinner in two minutes” without evidence. Paid platform authorisation is distinct from receiving a video file.
