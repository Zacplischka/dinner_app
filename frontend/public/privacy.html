Privacy policy

Updated 8 September 2026

YupCrew (formerly Dinder) helps friends choose where to eat, what to cook and what to watch. It is operated by Zac Plischka in Australia. This policy covers YupCrew and its legacy Dinder web addresses.

What we collect and why

  • Group activity. We process your chosen display name, session and participant identifiers, preferences, votes, results and group-order choices to run a shared session. Shared shopping lists also contain ingredient choices, changes and the names people use to claim items.
  • Optional Google sign-in. Google provides your account identifier, email address, name and profile picture. We use these through Supabase to sign you in, create your profile and support friends, requests and invitations. We do not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, contacts, calendars or files.
  • Location searches. When you choose current location and allow your browser to share it, we receive coordinates, which can be precise. You can enter a suburb or postcode instead. We send the location or search text to Google Places and Geocoding to find nearby venues and identify an area. A session can store its selected location.
  • Technical information. Our hosting and authentication services process information such as IP addresses, browser details, request URLs, timestamps and errors. Application logs can include account or session identifiers, search parameters and session-outcome counts. We use this information to operate, troubleshoot and protect the service and understand whether group decisions succeed.
  • Support requests. If you contact us, we receive your email address and what you send so we can respond.

What other people can see

Participants can see shared session information, including display names, choices and results. Anyone with a shopping-list link can view and change that list, including item claims and shopper names. Share session codes and list links only with people you trust.

If you sign in, another signed-in user who knows your exact email address can find your profile. Your name, profile picture and email address are included in friend-search results and relevant friend, request and invitation information. Guest sessions do not require a Google account.

Service providers and external links

We use Railway for hosting, Supabase for authentication and stored account/social data, and Cloudflare for domain routing, site delivery and recipe images. These providers process information needed to deliver those services. Google processes sign-in and location requests. They may process or store information outside Australia, including in the United States and other countries where they operate.

Restaurant comparisons use Apify to retrieve public venue and menu information from delivery services. Recipe and shopping features can send recipe searches or ingredient details to Spoonacular and Woolworths. These requests do not need your Google account details. Images loaded from external providers, such as movie posters and profile pictures, also disclose normal web-request information to their hosts.

When you follow a restaurant, delivery, grocery or streaming link, that service's privacy policy applies. YupCrew does not take payment or place an order on your behalf.

We do not sell personal information. The current app does not include advertising trackers. We do not use your Google account information, votes or other personal information to train AI models; AI tools used to prepare recipe content are separate from your use of the app.

Browser storage

We use local storage to keep you signed in and remember your shopper name, and session storage to restore a tab's group session and cooking progress. Our infrastructure providers may use cookies or similar technology for authentication and security. You can clear site data in your browser; this can sign you out and remove your ability to rejoin as the same participant. Clearing browser data does not delete your stored account.

How long information stays

Live group-session data expires after 30 minutes without qualifying activity; actions such as joining or submitting choices extend that period. Shopping lists expire seven days after creation. These limits apply to the live records, not every copy in browser storage, operational logs or provider backups.

Account profiles, friendships and invitation records are stored persistently and do not expire with a group session. You can remove friends and respond to requests in the app, or request account deletion below. Operational logs, backups and support correspondence have separate retention periods and may remain after live records expire or an account is deleted. We do not currently promise a single deletion deadline for those copies.

Your choices, access and deletion

You can use the core app as a guest, decline location permission, sign out, clear browser storage and remove YupCrew's connection in your Google account settings. Removing the Google connection stops that access but does not itself delete your YupCrew profile.

To request access to, correction of or deletion of your account information, or to raise a privacy concern, email yupcrewsupport@gmail.com. For account requests, contact us from the email used to sign in where possible. We may ask for enough information to verify ownership before making changes. Please do not send passwords or sign-in codes.

Account deletion includes the associated profile, friendships and invitations. Shared guest-session and list information may remain until its expiry; include the relevant session code or list link if you also want us to identify that information. We will explain any information that must be retained for security or legal reasons when responding to your request.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its date when our practices change. Material changes will also be highlighted in the app.
